What remodeling in Playa del Rey actually involves

Playa del Rey sits between Ballona Creek and LAX, and its housing splits by elevation. Lower Playa del Rey, around Del Rey Lagoon and along Culver Boulevard, Pershing Drive and Vista del Mar, was built on former wetland and dune. It holds beach houses, condo buildings and the closely built 1956 apartments locals call the Jungle, on Rees, Sunridge and Surf Streets. The Del Rey Hills east of Pershing Drive were developed from 1928 onward, with single-family homes on the bluffs looking out over the Ballona Wetlands and the bay.

Playa del Rey is part of the City of Los Angeles, so LADBS issues the building permit, but much of the neighborhood lies inside the California coastal zone. That adds a coastal review step through City Planning ahead of the building permit for many projects. The south end sits right against LAX, and the whole neighborhood lives in salt air. A good Playa del Rey remodel is planned around all three.

Permits & Local Rules

Who approves your project in Playa del Rey

Playa del Rey is a City of Los Angeles neighborhood, so LADBS issues the building permit. Much of it also sits inside the coastal zone, where a coastal development permit or an exemption is settled through City Planning first. Which one applies depends on the parcel and on how much of the house you change.

  • LADBS permits and inspections for structural, plumbing, electrical and mechanical work. Pulled in our name under License #1008892, never in yours.
  • Coastal zone status is checked first. We look the parcel up on ZIMAS, the City of LA parcel map, before design starts. If it sits inside the coastal zone, the coastal step is scheduled ahead of plan check.
  • Lower Playa del Rey is former wetland. Low-lying lots near the lagoon and Ballona Creek can carry flood-zone and soils questions that bluff lots do not. We check the designations before structural work is priced.
  • Condo work needs association approval on top of the city permit: plans, insurance naming the HOA, approved work hours and elevator or stair protection.
  • Pre-1978 homes require lead-safe work practices, which covers most of the neighborhood. We test before demolition or sanding begins.

Local Experience

Three things we plan for on every Playa del Rey job

01

Salt air shortens the life of the wrong product

Within a few blocks of the beach, standard exterior fasteners, flashing, hinges and light fixtures corrode early. We specify stainless or coated hardware and marine-rated exterior products in the written scope. They cost more at purchase and far less over the next decade.

02

LAX is a neighbor

The south end of Playa del Rey sits next to LAX, close enough that the airport bought out and cleared the Surfridge neighborhood just to the south. If earlier owners upgraded windows or insulation against noise, we identify them before demolition so a remodel does not quietly undo them, and we price acoustic glazing where it matters.

03

Bluff lots and lagoon lots are different builds

A house on the Del Rey Hills bluff and a cottage near the lagoon a few hundred yards away sit on very different ground. Slope, drainage, soils and access change the foundation and shoring scope. We price the ground your house actually stands on, which is why we look before we quote.

Common Questions

Playa del Rey FAQs

Do I need a coastal permit to remodel in Playa del Rey?

Possibly. Much of Playa del Rey, ZIP 90293, lies inside the California coastal zone, and there many projects must be cleared through Los Angeles City Planning, as a coastal development permit or an exemption, before LADBS issues the building permit. Interior work usually moves faster than exterior changes or additions. We check your parcel and scope at the estimate stage and tell you which track applies.

Who issues building permits in Playa del Rey?

The City of Los Angeles, through LADBS. Playa del Rey is a Los Angeles neighborhood, not a separate city like neighboring El Segundo. New Cali Construction pulls the permits and schedules the inspections under License #1008892.

What does salt air mean for my remodel?

It changes the specification, mainly for anything exterior or exposed: fasteners, flashing, hinges, railings, light fixtures and window hardware. Standard products corrode early within a few blocks of the ocean. We write coastal-grade materials into the scope so the price you sign covers the version that lasts.

How much does a remodel cost in Playa del Rey?

Planning ranges match the rest of the Westside: roughly $15,000 to $35,000 for a guest bathroom and $40,000 to $120,000 for a full kitchen. What moves a Playa del Rey project is coastal review time, coastal-grade materials and, on bluff lots, foundation and access work. Each is priced in the written scope before you sign.
