Service Areas

Local pages, not a list of city names

A remodel in Malibu and a remodel in Hawthorne have almost nothing in common once you get past the tile. These pages cover what changes: who issues the permit, what the houses are built like, and what we plan for before day one.

Culver City

Our home city and our office address. Culver City permits through its own building department, not LADBS, and runs its own ADU ordinance. We are ten minutes from that counter.

View Culver City →

Santa Monica

Its own Building and Safety Division, its own ADU rules and one of the tougher plan checks on the Westside. We price the Santa Monica version, not the City of LA version.

View Santa Monica →

Venice

Nearly every Venice house sits in the coastal zone, so a building permit needs coastal clearance first. We map that track before you pay an architect.

View Venice →

Mar Vista

Opening up post-war floor plans, adding second bathrooms, and turning alley garages into permitted ADUs - a mile and a half from our Culver City shop.

View Mar Vista →

Playa Vista

Condo and townhome remodels run the way Playa Vista actually requires them - HOA submittal first, elevator and loading dock booked, neighbors kept out of it.

View Playa Vista →

West LA

Sawtelle bungalows, Rancho Park traditionals and mid-rise condos - three very different builds, three very different remodels.

View West LA →

Marina del Rey

Waterfront condos and townhomes - county permits, association approvals and materials chosen for salt air, not for a showroom.

View Marina del Rey →

Beverly Hills

A city with its own building department, its own site rules and its own expectations of finish - all three planned for before we start.

View Beverly Hills →

Pacific Palisades

Ground-up rebuilds, smoke and heat damage renovation, and remodeling on the blocks the fire never reached. One licensed design-build contractor for all three.

View Pacific Palisades →

Westchester

Post-war Kentwood and Osage tract homes - the best ADU lots on the Westside, and a soundproofing package you should not tear out by accident.

View Westchester →

El Segundo

Small lots, older homes and a city building department that knows every street - El Segundo projects are won on planning, not muscle.

View El Segundo →

Malibu

Coastal permits, septic-served lots and fire-zone materials - Malibu punishes contractors who treat it like the rest of LA.

View Malibu →

Inglewood

1920s Spanish, post-war bungalows and income property - remodels priced for what the house is actually for.

View Inglewood →

Bel Air

Hillside estates on canyon roads - where access, engineering and review add more to a project than the finishes do.

View Bel Air →

Hawthorne

Post-war South Bay homes on straightforward lots - the best value-per-dollar ADU and addition work we do.

View Hawthorne →

Mid City

1920s Spanish and Craftsman homes with details worth saving - and older systems that need replacing behind them.
